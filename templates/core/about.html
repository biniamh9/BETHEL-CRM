Our pastor

Pastor Abraham Yosief

Pastor Abraham has served in Christian ministry across East Africa, Europe, and North America for more than five decades.

His ministry journey includes pastoral and regional leadership in Addis Ababa, evangelism and discipleship work throughout Eritrea and Ethiopia, pastoral service in Nairobi and London, and leadership among Eritrean evangelical church fellowships in Europe and North America.

Through seasons of sacrifice—including imprisonment for preaching the Gospel—he has remained steadfast in his calling. Today, he shepherds Bethel’s congregation with cross-cultural wisdom, a heart for discipleship, and a deep commitment to abundant life in Christ.
