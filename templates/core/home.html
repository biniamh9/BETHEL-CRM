About us

An anchor for Eritrean and East African families

For more than a decade we have been a place where families grow spiritually, children receive steady formation, young people find mentors, and newcomers find their first trusted community in a new country.

We serve families across Atlanta, Tucker, Stone Mountain, Clarkston, Lilburn and Decatur, offering family and marriage counseling, crisis support, new immigrant support, and weekly programs for children and youth.
